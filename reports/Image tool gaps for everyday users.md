# Fit forms first, then speak Urdu

The strongest, best-evidenced gap for Image Stacker's users is not creative. It is **getting a photo, signature or scan accepted by an official portal**: exam, bank, visa and ID portals demand a file inside an exact KB window and pixel size, applicants get rejected and confused, and the market answers with dozens of ad-heavy, mostly upload-based "compress to 20 KB" sites while the big compressors (TinyPNG, iLoveIMG, Squoosh) cannot target a size at all. The recommended first batch is therefore an "applicant pack" (fit to exact KB and pixels, signature cleanup, safe-to-share metadata stripping, and HEIC/WebP intake), together with proper Arabic and Urdu (Nastaliq) text for captions and quote cards. That second pillar serves the religious-study and community audience directly, and the evidence shows mainstream tools handle it badly. Phone-to-scan cleanup, size-capped A4 PDFs and passport photos come next. Microsoft Lens has retired and left a real gap there, but these need more engineering and maintained spec tables. AI features (background removal, upscaling) are feasible in the browser but crowded, heavy on phones and full of licence traps, so they rank low. Two caveats apply throughout. The researchers retrieved **almost no direct Reddit, Quora or app-store review text**, and **no search-volume data at all**, so demand is inferred from official specs, tool proliferation, vendor-reported user counts and review aggregators. Treat the ranking as a well-reasoned hypothesis to test, not a measured market map.

## Fifteen ideas, ranked by evidence, browser feasibility and fit

The ranking weighs three things equally: how strong and direct the evidence of pain is, whether the feature can run fully client-side on a mid-range phone without large model downloads, and how much it reuses what Image Stacker already has. That existing base is a canvas render pipeline with PNG, JPG and PDF export (jsPDF), JSZip batch output, pdf.js page import, margin auto-trim, captions, a text watermark, the blur, pixelate and box redaction layer, and Web Share. Effort figures come from the research notes' own estimates for a team that already ships a canvas tool. They are inferred, not measured.

### 1. Fit to an exact KB window and pixel size

**Evidence.** This has the best evidence in the whole study because governments write the requirement down. SSC CGL 2026 requires the **signature as a JPEG of 10–20 KB**, and states that a wrong format or size and "blurred or miniature signatures" are rejected ([Physics Wallah](https://www.pw.live/ssc/exams/ssc-cgl-photo-and-signature-size-2026)). Bank recruitment notices use the same pattern of a KB window plus a pixel size ([Punjab & Sind Bank PDF](https://punjabandsind.bank.in/system/uploads/document/2150_2024083018282832730.pdf); [RBI notice](https://website.rbi.org.in/documents/87730/39016390/RPJECE07012019_AN1.pdf)). The US DS-160 photo must be a **JPEG of 240 KB or less, square, between 600×600 and 1200×1200 px**, and the State Department's own tool only crops ([travel.state.gov](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/photos/digital-image-requirements.html.)). Applicants are visibly confused. One asked on Careers360 whether a 413×513 px, 284 KB photo would be rejected: "I am very confused" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-are-pixels-important-for-the-photo-and-signature-in-ssc-gd-form-or-just-the-size-should-be-20kb-to-50kb-size-my-photo-is-413513-pixels-and-size-284kb-is-it-ok-or-my-form-will-get-rejected-if-i-upload-it-please-reply-and-help-me-clear-this-doubt-i-am-very-confused)). Guides explain that users mix up pixel errors with KB errors, and that portals also reject files that are **too light** ([ilovexams](https://ilovexams.in/resizer/guides/upload-error-messages-explained/)). Supply is the demand proxy: at least ten sites run "compress image to 20 KB" landing pages ([SmallSEOTools](https://smallseotools.com/compress-image-to-20kb/); [11zon](https://imagecompressor.11zon.com/en/image-compressor/compress-image-to-20kb-online); [Pi7](https://image.pi7.org/compress-image-to-20kb)), plus a cluster of Indian exam-resizer domains ([sizetokb.in](https://sizetokb.in/); [examphotoresize.in](https://examphotoresize.in/)), and one resizer app claims 5M+ users (secondary source) ([tamindir](https://www.tamindir.com/indir/image-size-photo-resizer/amp/)).

**Target users.** Job, exam and visa applicants in India, Pakistan and the Gulf, and the cyber-café operators, coaching centres and family members who do the uploads for them.

**What existing tools get wrong.** TinyPNG caps free use at 20 images and 5 MB each, and has no size target ([Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/tinypng)). Squoosh does one image at a time with no KB target, and Google has archived its CLI and lib ([@squoosh/lib README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@squoosh/lib@0.5.3/README.md)). The SEO-farm sites mostly upload files, and they only shrink: almost none handle the minimum-KB case. Presets go stale. The SSC photo is now captured live, and third-party sites disagree on SSC pixel sizes (200×230 against 275×354 or 280×340) ([onlineresult.in](https://www.onlineresult.in/tools/exam-photo-resizer?preset=ssc); [globaldrishti](https://globaldrishti.in/tools/photo-signature-resizer/)).

**Differentiator.** Take the spec as input ("min 10 KB, max 20 KB, 140×60, JPG") and solve for it in one step, including the too-light case. Every preset carries its source link and a "last checked" date, and every value can be edited. The tool works offline, handles batches, and puts a pass/fail check next to each file.

**Feasibility and risk.** Fully client-side. Resize to the target pixels, then binary-search `canvas.toBlob` JPEG quality, aiming 2–3% under the maximum to absorb the 1000-versus-1024 KB ambiguity ([pythonware](https://www.pythonware.com/compress-image-to-20kb)). If the file is still under the minimum at full quality, raise the dimensions or pad. Padding meets the rule but is a hack, so flag it in the UI. There is no licence risk. The real risk is maintaining presets: a wrong preset causes the rejection the tool exists to prevent.

**Fit.** Very high. It is a new output mode next to the existing WhatsApp-size fitting, and it reuses trim, auto-margin removal, PDF page import (many signatures arrive as scanned PDFs) and Web Share.

### 2. Signature cleanup and extraction

**Evidence.** Stated rejection causes include blur, wrong size and **too much blank space around the signature** ([signatureresize.in](https://signatureresize.in/signature-upload-errors-and-fixes); [Physics Wallah](https://www.pw.live/ssc/exams/ssc-cgl-photo-and-signature-size-2026)). The free "remove background from signature" tools belong to AI photo-editor brands (Pixelcut, Picsart, BeautyPlus) that upload the image and push app upsells ([Pixelcut](https://www.pixelcut.ai/hi-in/background-remover/remove-background-from-signature); [Picsart](https://picsart.com/ar/background-remover/signature/)). That means uploading a legally meaningful image to do something that needs no AI.

**Differentiator, feasibility and fit.** A local pipeline of greyscale, Otsu or adaptive threshold, whitened or transparent paper, optional black or blue ink recolour, and auto-crop to the ink, followed by idea #1's size solver. It takes about a day with no model and no licence risk. The existing auto-trim code (`src/lib/trim.ts`) already detects plain margins, so cropping to the ink is a small extension. A transparent signature PNG is also useful for signing PDFs, which ties into the PDF features.

### 3. Safe-to-share: location warning and metadata strip

**Evidence.** WhatsApp's standard photo send strips GPS. **"Send as document" keeps all metadata**, and people choose document mode precisely to avoid quality loss ([fast.io, citing third-party testers; medium confidence](https://www.fast.io/resources/whatsapp-telegram-signal-photo-metadata-preservation.md)). On iPhone, location can be removed only per share, and the setting is not remembered, which frustrates users on Apple's forum ([Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256092610); [Apple guide](https://support.apple.com/en-om/guide/iphone/iphf28f17237/ios)). There is no official WhatsApp documentation, and behaviour may differ by platform and version.

**Differentiator and fit.** Image Stacker's exports are re-rendered on canvas, so they already drop EXIF. Today that protection is invisible and accidental. The feature makes it visible: read EXIF on import and show "This photo contains a location" with a rough place when one exists. Then offer a lossless "strip only" mode that removes the JPEG APP1 segment without re-encoding, applying orientation first, for families who want full quality as a document. Effort is 1–2 days with no licence risk. It is also the cheapest credible proof of the "nothing leaves your phone" message.

### 4. Open anything: HEIC, WebP and AVIF intake

**Evidence.** In 2020, US AP exam submissions failed because the College Board portal could not handle iPhone HEIC photos. The fix was to switch the camera to "Most Compatible" ([iDrop News](https://www.idropnews.com/news/why-iphones-suddenly-caused-students-to-fail-ap-tests/136323); [TidBITS](https://tidbits.com/2020/05/21/take-a-heic-make-sure-ap-and-other-test-uploads-work-from-your-iphone-and-ipad/)). Platforms were still adding HEIC support in 2025 ([CartonCloud](https://cartoncloud.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/KB2/blog/2025/01/15/581926928/Photos+on+Purchase+Orders+now+supports+HEIC+image+uploads+via+the+webapp.)). WebP downloads that Windows Photos and older editors will not open are a documented annoyance ([Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/a/6473151); [AskWoody](https://www.askwoody.com/forums/topic/seeing-the-webp-picture-file-used-more-often)).

**Why it matters for Image Stacker specifically.** **HEIC decodes natively only in Safari 17+. Chrome, Chrome Android and Edge cannot decode it** ([caniuse](https://caniuse.com/heif)). Image Stacker loads files through an `<img>` element, so an iPhone HEIC file opened in Chrome on Windows or Android most likely fails at intake today. This is an inference from the code, not a tested bug. WebP and AVIF already decode natively, so they only need a clear "save as JPG" path.

**Feasibility and risk.** Lazy-load a libheif WebAssembly decoder (about 1–2 MB, unmeasured) only when a .heic file arrives, and use native decoding on Safari. Effort is 2–4 days. Licence risk: the research did not verify the decoder's licence. libheif is generally understood to be LGPL, which is workable when it is loaded as a separate module, but confirm this before building.

### 5. Arabic and Urdu (Nastaliq) text for captions and quote cards

**Evidence.** The evidence is qualitative but consistent. Canva watermarks Pro elements for free users ([Canva Help](https://www.canva.com/help/article/watermarks-design/)). Its Urdu font list is mostly Naskh-style faces ([Canva on X, 2021](https://twitter.com/canva/status/1385866601833041921)). A 2026 guide says Nastaliq users must upload custom fonts, which is a Pro feature ([urdufontss.net, commercial source](https://urdufontss.net/how-to-add-urdu-fonts-in-canva/)). A whole category of "Arabic text fixer" sites exists to pre-shape text for Canva, Cricut and Affinity ([arabictextfixer.com](https://www.arabictextfixer.com/); [rtlfixer.com](https://rtlfixer.com/this-is-how-to-write-in-arabic-hebrew-urdu-and-more-in-affinity-designer-affinity-photo-and-affinity-publisher/)). Photoshop shows disconnected Arabic letters unless a hidden composer setting is changed, and the problem was still being reported in 2026 ([Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-712/i-have-a-problem-with-unified-text-engine-1551690)). The Urdu-poetry-on-photo apps are low quality. One is rated 3.8 from 24 ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1282880434)), and a Chrome extension's feedback lists "poor export quality and presence of a watermark" ([chrome-stats](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.ionicframework.urduonpicture524687)). The Canva behaviour is unverified for 2026, and Islamic quote-card demand was not researched directly.

**Target users.** Religious study circles (Quran, hadith and poetry snippets), community announcements, and Urdu-speaking families and teachers.

**Feasibility.** Good, and no WASM is needed. Canvas `direction = "rtl"` has been Baseline since May 2022, and the browser's own engine does the shaping ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/CanvasRenderingContext2D/direction)). Load Noto Nastaliq Urdu or Noto Naskh Arabic lazily ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Nastaliq+Urdu/about)), set direction from the first strong character, use a line height of about 2×, and **never apply letter-spacing or forced ligature or feature settings to RTL runs**. Forced settings broke Nastaliq shaping in Chrome 138 on Discourse ([meta.discourse.org](https://meta.discourse.org/t/discourse-font-rendering/378897)), and letter-spacing reversed RTL text in Fabric.js ([Q&A mirror](https://answer.demo.spiceai.io/questions/10010000000036142)). Test on iOS Safari, because WebKit has had Nastaliq positioning bugs ([WebKit 255972](https://onbugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=255972)). Mixed Arabic and English punctuation is the most common failure and needs RLM/LRM handling ([dtplabs](https://www.dtplabs.com/blog/rtl-typography-complete-guide-arabic-hebrew-farsi)).

**Licence risk.** Awami Nastaliq is under the SIL Open Font License. Jameel Noori Nastaleeq, the most popular style in Pakistan, has unclear licensing and should not be bundled ([Wikipedia: Awami Nastaliq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awami_Nastaliq); [MediaWiki discussion](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Topic:Rj1bvuays5l3acux)).

**Fit.** Very high. Captions, the watermark and image numbers currently draw with a single Latin UI font (IBM Plex Sans with system fallbacks) and no explicit canvas direction. RTL-aware captions are a 2–4 day upgrade to existing code. Quote and announcement cards (background, text block, attribution, 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1080×1920) add about a week and become a new reason to open the app.

### 6. Phone photo to clean scan

**Evidence.** **Microsoft Lens, the main free scanner with no ads or watermark, was retired.** Scanning stopped on 9 March 2026, and its replacement, the OneDrive scanner, saves only to OneDrive ([Microsoft Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-GB/lens/retirement-of-microsoft-lens); [BGR](https://bgr.com/1936952/microsoft-lens-app-ios-android-being-retired/)). CamScanner's free tier stamps a watermark on every export and plays 15–30 second ads ([Unstar, vendor-run comparison](https://unstar.app/blog/adobe-scan-camscanner-microsoft-lens-genius-scan-swiftscan-pdf-scanner-apps-ranked-2026); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/210426/CamScanner/reviews/)). Its 2019 malware incident affected an app with 100M+ downloads ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2019/08/android-camscanner-malware.html)). Adobe Scan requires sign-in before the first page ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/adobe-scan-camscanner-microsoft-lens-genius-scan-swiftscan-pdf-scanner-apps-ranked-2026)).

**Feasibility, risk and fit.** jscanify on OpenCV.js does page detection and perspective correction ([jscanify README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/jscanify@1.4.2/README.md)). Add draggable corners, shadow removal and adaptive black-and-white for about 3–5 days. The risks are the several-MB OpenCV.js download (lazy-load it), weak detection on cluttered or low-contrast backgrounds (untested), and jscanify's licence, which was not checked. Fit is good: cleaned pages feed straight into stacking, numbering, the PDF export and idea #7. Note that a browser scanner works from photos taken with the camera app or from a live `getUserMedia` view. It will feel less polished than a native scanner.

### 7. Images to A4 PDF under a size cap

**Evidence.** Portals and email both impose file caps, and the free web PDF tools limit daily tasks and require uploads. Reviewers complain about iLovePDF's caps and ads, and raise privacy concerns about Smallpdf and iLovePDF ([Capterra iLovePDF](https://capterra.com/p/173963/iLovePDF/reviews/); [Capterra Smallpdf](https://www.capterra.com/p/172606/Smallpdf/reviews/); [G2](https://g2.com/products/ilovepdf/reviews_and_filters?page=3)).

**Fit and effort.** Image Stacker already exports PDF with jsPDF, but at stack-sized pages. The addition is one page per image on A4 or Letter with margins, plus idea #1's solver applied across JPEG quality and DPI to land under a target such as 200 KB or 1 MB. Effort is 1–2 days, with no new dependency.

### 8. Passport and ID photo maker

**Evidence.** Indian embassy notices make ICAO-compliant self-uploaded photos (630×810 px, white background, unaltered) **mandatory from 1 December 2025** ([Indian Embassy Doha](https://audit.indianembassyqatar.gov.in/users/assets/pdf/whatsnew/Doha_30nov2025_1.pdf)). Pakistan's CNIC and NICOP uploads are 350×467 px, with dedicated commercial pages for them ([visafoto](https://visafoto.com/pk-cnic-350x467-px-photo)). Many apps lock background removal and compliance checks behind payment, though these sources are vendors ranking themselves ([PhotoAiD](https://photoaid.com/blog/free-passport-photo-apps/)). UAE sources contradict each other on size ([photogov](https://photogov.net/knowledge/requirements/uae-visa-photo-requirements/); [passlens](https://passlens.com/blog/uae-visa-photo-requirements-guide)).

**Feasibility and risk.** MediaPipe Face Landmarker runs in the browser and gives crown and chin positions to scale and centre the head ([Google](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker/web_js)). Add presets, a 4×6 print sheet and the KB solver for about 4–6 days. Background replacement needs a segmentation model (see #13) and conflicts with the "unedited" rules in the US and India, so brighten the background only and keep face pixels untouched. The main risk is liability and maintenance: a confidently wrong preset means a rejected visa. Ship only presets with an official source link.

### 9. WhatsApp QR kit

**Evidence.** The pain is strong and well documented. QRCG has a Trustpilot score of 1.3/5 from 9,335 reviews ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.qr-code-generator.com)), and "free" dynamic codes are deactivated after 10–14 day trials, in one case after 10,000 flyers were printed ([Good Men Project](https://goodmenproject.com/business-ethics-2/they-printed-10000-flyers-then-their-qr-codes-went-dark/); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/177261/QR-Code-Generator-Pro/reviews/)). One secondhand figure puts "free QR code generator" at 110K monthly searches ([txtimpact](https://www.txtimpact.com/blog/best-free-qr-code-generator)). That is the only search-volume number in the research.

**What gets it wrong, and the differentiator.** "No signup, no expiry" is now table stakes ([OpenQR](https://openqr.tech/); [FreeQR](https://www.freeqr.world/)), and WhatsApp QR generators are mostly B2B lead magnets ([Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/free-whatsapp-qr-code-generator/); [MSG91](https://msg91.com/in/free-whatsapp-link-generator)). The differentiator is integration rather than the claim. A static `wa.me` code with a prefilled message ([Saysimple](https://helpcenter.saysimple.com/whatsapp-management/whatsapp-click-to-chat)), WiFi and contact codes, and an optional logo, all placed into a captioned flyer or Status image and shared straight to WhatsApp. Effort is 2–3 days. The QR encoder library's licence was not checked. Fit is medium, because the input is text rather than images.

### 10. Auto-detect faces and phone numbers for redaction

**Evidence.** There is little direct demand evidence. The case rests on fit and privacy: privacy-first launches lead with on-device processing ([BG0](https://github.com/opencoredev/bg0/wiki)), and families share children's photos and screenshots of group chats that contain phone numbers.

**Feasibility and fit.** BlazeFace short-range runs in about 3–7 ms on a Pixel 6 ([Google AI Edge](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_detector)), and Tesseract.js word boxes plus regular expressions find phone numbers and emails. Detected boxes become suggestions in the existing blur, pixelate and box layer, which survives trims and exports. Effort is 2–5 days. The BlazeFace model-card licence was not verified, and the code samples are Apache 2.0.

### 11. Local OCR: copy text out of images and PDF pages

**Evidence.** OCR is a common paywall. CamScanner charges $4.99/month for it ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/adobe-scan-camscanner-microsoft-lens-genius-scan-swiftscan-pdf-scanner-apps-ranked-2026)), and Adobe Scan's free tier stops at 25 OCR pages ([Adobe docs](https://www.adobe.com/devnet-docs/adobescan/ios/en/subscriptions.html)). Mainstream apps are weak on non-Latin scripts.

**Feasibility and risk.** Tesseract.js is mature and its language packs are about 0.7–1 MB each. But it was **the worst engine on Urdu Nastaliq in a 2025 benchmark (CER 1.42), and poor on Naskh (CER 0.955)** ([arXiv 2505.13943](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.13943v2)). PP-OCRv5's Arabic-script and Devanagari models run in the browser through onnxruntime-web ([ppu-paddle-ocr](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/ppu-paddle-ocr@5.1.1/README.md)), but their Nastaliq accuracy is unbenchmarked. Expect usable English, Hindi and printed Arabic, and label Urdu "experimental". Effort is 2–3 days for Tesseract.js plus 3–5 days for an optional PP-OCRv5 engine. This ranks middling because the audience most likely to want it (Urdu readers) would get the weakest results.

### 12. Logo watermark with position, opacity and batch

**Evidence and fit.** Local batch watermarkers exist (SmartWatermark, with a $29 one-time Pro tier; [hunted.space](https://www.hunted.space/product/smartwatermark)). The Google bulk-watermark add-on wants Drive access and its Chrome listing was last updated in 2019 ([Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/bulk_watermark_for_images_photos/204753129152)). Watermarkly writes Canva watermark guides, which suggests people improvise ([Watermarkly](https://watermarkly.com/blog/how-to-add-a-watermark-in-canva/)). Small sellers and teachers protecting worksheets are the users. Image Stacker already has a text watermark and ZIP export, so a logo image, position, opacity and a per-image (not per-stack) mode take about 2–4 days.

### 13. Background removal

**Evidence.** remove.bg's free output is capped at **0.25 MP** ([remove.bg](https://remove.bg/help/a/is-remove-bg-free-)). PhotoRoom draws billing-after-cancellation complaints ([eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/photoroom-reviews); [Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/www.photoroom.com?page=5)). Canva locks its remover behind Pro ([cutout.pro, competitor](https://www.cutout.pro/remove-background/background-remover-canva)).

**Why it ranks low.** The category is commoditised: many "no signup, runs in browser" removers launch to single-digit upvotes ([hunted.space CutOut](https://www.hunted.space/product/cutout-free-background-remover); [topai.tools](https://topai.tools/s/no-signup-background-remover)). The licences are traps. **BRIA RMBG-1.4 and 2.0 are non-commercial** ([RMBG-1.4](https://huggingface.co/briaai/RMBG-1.4); [RMBG-2.0](https://huggingface.co/briaai/RMBG-2.0)), and IMG.LY's library is AGPL-3.0 ([GitHub](https://github.com/imgly/background-removal-js)). Stock BiRefNet-lite ONNX exports run out of memory in ONNX Runtime Web ([CoderViking](https://huggingface.co/CoderViking/birefnet-lite-onnx)). One self-report gives **about 12 s per image on mid-range hardware** ([Show HN](https://hn.svelte.dev/item/45940793)), and iOS Safari kills the tab without an error when it runs out of memory ([WebKit 300782](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=300782)). Build it only later, as a step inside passport photos or product stacks, with MODNet or a licence-checked BiRefNet re-export, and plan for 1–2 weeks.

### 14. Before/after wipe and photos-to-short-video

The only evidence is that existing before/after tools output interactive web sliders that WhatsApp cannot show ([imgi](https://alternativeto.net/software/imgi-co/about/); [Wix app](https://www.wix.com/app-market/before-after-photo-slider-globalentryfast)), and that ezgif is upload-based and desktop-oriented ([ezgif](https://ezgif.com/maker)). Side-by-side already exists in Image Stacker. The new part, an animated wipe or slideshow exported as MP4, needs video encoding, which the research did not cost. Users like salons, tailors, cleaners and tutors showing results are plausible, but none were evidenced.

### 15. Quiz and lesson card sets

This is a pure hypothesis. The research found only that question papers circulate as WhatsApp photos ([National Herald](https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/amp/story/national/mp-teacher-suspended-for-sharing-class-10-question-paper-on-whatsapp)), plus vendor marketing ([to-teach.ai](https://to-teach.ai/blog/teaching-the-future-progressive-tense-in-an-engaging-way/turn-your-image-into-a-multiple-choice-quiz)). It ranks last on evidence but costs 2–3 days because it reuses numbering, captions and carousel slicing. It is worth testing with a handful of teachers before any build.

**Considered and parked.** Screenshot beautifiers and annotation tools are well served for free ([Screenhance](https://screenhance.com/screenshot-annotation-tool); [CleanSnap](https://10015.io/product/cleansnap)), and annotation would take 1–2 weeks. Upscaling is feasible with BSD-licensed Real-ESRGAN weights, but heavy on phones, and popular community models are non-commercial ([HF AI_upscalers](https://huggingface.co/easygoing0114/AI_upscalers)). Palette extraction is speculative, and favicons serve developers.

## Privacy alone will not win, but verifiable privacy plus WhatsApp output can

"Runs locally, nothing uploaded" is no longer a differentiator on its own. The category is saturated with near-identical suites of 20–100 tools ([privacyfirst.tools](https://privacyfirst.tools/); [NoUploadTools directory](https://nouploadtools.com/directory?cat=security-privacy)). A 2025 "privacy-first, no uploads" compressor got **1 point on Hacker News** ([HN 46042122](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46042122)). Technical users doubt the claim can be verified: "one can never be sure that the image actually stays within the browser" ([HN 30045901](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=30045901)). What the incumbents get wrong is their business model, not their capability. Pixlr allows **3 saves a day** ([Atlas Cloud](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/tips/pixlr-alternative)). Kapwing watermarks every free export because "every project… costs us money to process" ([Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/help/our-watermark-policy/)). Scanner and AI apps show the paywall only after the work is done. A client-side app costs nothing per use, so it can credibly promise **no caps, no account and no watermark, ever**, which server-based rivals cannot afford.

Two consequences follow for Image Stacker. First, make privacy provable rather than just claimed. A "works in airplane mode" badge answers the top HN objection, since the PWA already caches. The location warning from idea #3 makes the protection visible on the user's own photo. Open-sourcing helped VERT ([VERT](https://vert.sh/privacy/)). Second, the uncontested ground is **WhatsApp as the destination**. None of the traffic leaders (iLoveIMG at about 50M visits a month, Photopea at about 17M; [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/iloveimg.com/overview/); [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/photopea.com/)) is designed around chat limits, Status sizes or the share sheet. For distribution, the notes suggest one crawlable route per tool (for example /fit/ssc-signature or /qr/whatsapp) to catch single-purpose searches, and chaining tools inside the app for retention. They also suggest a share-sheet link rather than a watermark on exported images, because watermarks are exactly what users flee ([Griddy reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/griddy-split-photo-grid-posts/id1114106704)).

## Evidence is strong on specs and supply, thin on user voice

The evidence is uneven, and the ranking should be read with that in mind. **Strongest:** official documents (SSC, RBI, bank notices, travel.state.gov, Indian embassy ICAO notices, the Microsoft Lens retirement page, caniuse, MDN), vendor policy pages that document their own limits (Canva, Kapwing, remove.bg, Adobe Scan), and peer-reviewed or primary technical sources (the arXiv OCR benchmark, model cards, WebKit bugs). **Medium:** review aggregators (Trustpilot, Capterra, G2) and forum threads (Adobe Community, Apple Community, Microsoft Q&A, Discourse). These are real user voices, but self-selected and mostly about Western products. **Weak or biased:** competitor comparison blogs (Unstar, PhotoAiD, ConvertSafe, nexasphere), AI-generated review summaries, and self-reported user counts. These are flagged where they carry a claim.

The gaps are material. The searches returned **almost no Reddit, Quora or Play Store review text**, so complaint wording comes from aggregators and help forums. There is **no keyword-volume or Google Trends data** apart from one secondhand QR figure. Nothing came from South Asian or Middle Eastern user communities themselves: no Pakistani or Indian teacher forums, madrasa or halaqa groups, or Urdu Facebook groups. There is no survey of how many people avoid uploading IDs. Several official specs (NADRA, UAE ICP, UK) could not be fetched, and pixel specs conflict between third-party sites. **No performance numbers exist for any model on a named mid-range Android phone or on iPhone Safari**, so all AI effort estimates are guesses. Effort estimates throughout are the researchers' inferences, not measurements. Finally, demand for ideas #10, #14 and #15 is essentially unevidenced. They are in the list because they are cheap and fit well, not because users asked for them.

## Recommended first batch and what to settle first

**First batch, about 2–3 weeks: ideas #1, #2, #3 and #4, plus RTL-aware captions from #5.** The first four form a coherent "applicant pack" that shares one size-solver core and serves the best-evidenced pain. They need no ML models and carry no licence risk except the HEIC decoder. The RTL captions are a small upgrade to existing code that serves the core community audience, and they fix something that probably renders imperfectly today. **Second batch:** the scan cleanup and A4 size-capped PDF (#6, #7), quote and announcement cards (the rest of #5), and the WhatsApp QR kit (#9). **Hold** passport photos (#8) until a sourced preset table exists, and hold the AI features (#10, #11, #13) until they have been tested on real mid-range phones.

Settle these open questions before building. Which portals and countries get presets in the first release, and who re-checks them each exam cycle against official notices? Does the fit-to-size tool sit inside the existing stacker flow as an export target, or behind its own entry point and route for search discovery? Should the app pad files to meet a minimum KB, given that padding technically satisfies the portal but is a hack? Is the app staying closed-source or going open-source? That decides whether LGPL or AGPL components and non-commercial models are acceptable, and whether open-sourcing is used as a trust signal. How much lazy-loaded download (fonts, a HEIC decoder, OpenCV.js, models) is acceptable on prepaid mobile data, and should the app ask before downloading? Can a quick round of validation happen first, for example five to ten conversations with exam applicants, a cyber-café operator, a study-circle organiser and a small seller, plus a keyword-tool pull for "compress to 20 kb", "signature resize", "urdu poetry on photo" and "whatsapp qr"? That would fill the biggest gap in this research.

## Conclusion

The research changes the frame for Image Stacker. The most valuable next tools are not more ways to make images look good. They are tools that make an image **acceptable to a gatekeeper** (an exam portal, an embassy, a bank) or **readable to a community** (Urdu and Arabic text that shapes correctly). Both are jobs where the incumbents fail for structural reasons. Server tools cannot afford unlimited free use, and mainstream design tools treat RTL and Nastaliq as an afterthought. A client-side PWA has a cost advantage on the first and an engineering opening on the second.

The risk to manage is not technical, since almost everything in the first batch is a few days of canvas work. It is the accuracy of spec presets and the lack of direct user voice. One round of real conversations and keyword data would turn this ranking from a reasoned bet into a validated plan.
